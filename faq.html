FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to the things people often ask before booking. If your question isn't here, just get in touch.

Where do you work?

We service Lake Macquarie and Newcastle. If you're outside these areas, send us a message anyway and we'll let you know whether we can help.

How much do you charge?

Weekday sessions are $70 per hour with a 4-hour minimum booking ($280). If we need a little longer, extra time is added in 15-minute steps.

Weekend sessions are occasionally available at a higher rate, confirmed with you before you book.

See our pricing page for full details.

Do I need a consultation first?

Smaller jobs can be booked directly without a consultation.

Larger projects, such as a walk-in pantry, walk-in wardrobe, garage, whole-home organising or downsizing, start with a $70 in-home consultation of up to 60 minutes to assess your space and plan next steps.

If you book 8 or more hours within 30 days, we'll credit the consultation fee.

When and how do I pay?

Payment is settled at the end of your session. Your invoice is prepared and sent before we finish.

OSKO or direct deposit is preferred, and you can also pay online or tap to pay using Square.

Do I need to buy new products or storage containers?

Not necessarily. We're happy to work with what you already have, and we'll only suggest new products where they'll genuinely help.

If you don't have containers or organisers in place yet, it helps to have a budget in mind so we can suggest options that suit your space and what you're comfortable spending.

Products are charged separately from the hourly rate. We'll usually bring along options we think will work well in your space, and nothing is used until you've agreed to it.

What if the job takes longer than expected?

We'll check in with you around the halfway point on progress and whether extra time would help. Extra time is only added once you've agreed to it.

If more work remains at the end of your session, we'll leave your space tidy, give you a short list of what's left, and offer a time for a follow-up session.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Changes with less than 48 hours' notice, and missed appointments, incur a $70 late-change fee.

What checks and training do you have?

We hold current checks and training so you can feel confident having us in your home:

  • National Police Check
  • Working with Children Check
  • NDIS Worker Screening Check
  • NDIS Worker Orientation Completion Certificate
  • First aid and basic emergency life support certificates (HLTAID011 and HLTAID010)

Details are available on request.

Are you insured?

Yes. I hold current Professional Indemnity and Public Liability insurance. A certificate of currency is available on request.

Do you offer NDIS support?

Yes. NDIS funding can vary, and eligibility depends on your plan, your goals and the type of service being delivered.

We're happy to talk through the options with you. See our NDIS support page for more.

How do I get started?

Give us a call on 0494 912 884, or fill in the form on our contact page with your suburb and what you'd like help with.

We'll be in touch to talk through your space, your goals and how many hours you might need.

Still have a question?

Get in touch and we'll happily talk it through with you.
