Initial consultation Fixed fee

Every organising job starts with an in-home consultation. It's a relaxed 60-90 minute visit where we:

  • Talk through what's working and what isn't.
  • Look at your space, what you already have, and what can be reused.
  • Work out which organisers or containers would help, so we can suggest or bring them to the next session.

It's also your chance to get to know us and make sure you feel comfortable before deciding to go ahead. You're under no obligation to book a follow-up organising session.

The consultation is a separate fixed-fee service. If you book a four-hour or longer organising session within 30 days, we'll credit the consultation fee towards it.

Important note

NDIS funding can vary. NDIS eligibility depends on your plan, your goals, and the type of service being delivered. We’re happy to talk through the options with you.

What’s included in the hourly fee? The hourly fee covers organising time and support only. Products, storage containers, labels, and other organising materials are not included and will be charged separately. We’ll discuss and approve any purchases with you first.
