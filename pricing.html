Pricing

Simple, clear pricing.

One hourly rate for weekday sessions, a 4-hour minimum booking, and payment at the end of your session, so you always know what to expect. Weekend sessions are occasionally available at a higher rate, so just ask.

Organising, downsizing & family support Weekdays: $70/hour
  • $70 per hour on weekdays (Monday to Friday), with a 4-hour minimum booking ($280). If we need a little longer, extra time is added in 15-minute steps.

Smaller jobs can be booked directly without a consultation.

Larger projects—such as a walk-in pantry, walk-in wardrobe, garage, whole-home organising or downsizing—start with a $70 in-home consultation of up to 60 minutes to assess your space and plan next steps. Book 8 or more hours within 30 days and we'll credit the consultation fee.

Weekend sessions By arrangement

Weekend sessions are occasionally available at a higher hourly rate. The rate is confirmed with you before you book, so you always know what to expect.

Payment At end of session
  • Payment is settled at the end of your session. Your invoice is prepared and sent before we finish, so there is nothing to follow up afterwards.
  • The invoice shows the actual time worked at the agreed hourly rate ($70 per hour on weekdays), or the 4-hour minimum if that applies, plus any approved materials.
  • OSKO or direct deposit is preferred. You can also pay online or tap to pay using Square.
Extra time & follow-up sessions Agreed together
  • We'll check in with you around the halfway point on progress and whether extra time would help.
  • Extra time is only added once you've agreed to it, and is recorded on your invoice.
  • Sessions finish at the booked time unless extra time has been agreed and we're available. If more work remains, we'll leave your space tidy, give you a short list of what's left, and offer a time for a follow-up session.
Changes & cancellations 48 hours' notice

Please give at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a booking so we can offer the time to someone else. Cancellations or rescheduling with less than 48 hours' notice, and missed appointments, incur a $70 late-change fee.

NDIS support Hourly / custom

Important note

NDIS funding can vary. NDIS eligibility depends on your plan, your goals, and the type of service being delivered. We’re happy to talk through the options with you.

What’s included in the hourly fee? The hourly rate covers organising time and support only. Products, storage containers, labels, and other organising materials are not included and will be charged separately. We’ll discuss and approve any purchases with you first. Rubbish removal and donation drop-offs are also not included.
